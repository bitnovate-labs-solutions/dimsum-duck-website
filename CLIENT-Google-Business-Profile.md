# Dim Sum Duck — Google Maps listing (for the restaurant)

This is a short guide for Dim Sum Duck to claim and complete your **Google Business Profile**. That is what makes the restaurant appear on **Google Search** and **Google Maps**.

The website (dimsumduck.com) cannot do this part. Only someone who owns or manages the restaurants can.

**Why this matters:** people searching “dim sum duck” often see **Dim Dou Duck in Kuala Lumpur** instead of you. Google Maps may also show nothing, or an old name such as “Dim Sum & Duck”. Claiming both London listings, with the details below, is how you fix that.

---

## What you need

- A Google account (Gmail is fine)
- Access to the restaurant phone, or someone on site for a video check
- About 20–30 minutes per location, plus waiting time for Google to verify

You need **two listings** — one for each restaurant.

## Step 1 — See if a listing already exists

1. Open [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps).
2. Search:
   - `Dim Sum Duck 124 King's Cross Road London`
   - `Dim Sum Duck 186 Pentonville Road London`
   - Also try `Dim Sum & Duck King's Cross`
3. If you see **your** restaurant:
   - Open it.
   - Look for **Claim this business**, **Own this business?**, or **Manage now**.
   - Follow the prompts.
4. If the name is wrong (for example “Dim Sum & Duck”), still claim it, then change the name to **Dim Sum Duck** after you have access.
5. If nothing appears, go to Step 2.

Do **not** create a second listing if one already exists for that address. Duplicate listings confuse Google.

---

## Step 2 — Create a listing (only if none exists)

1. Go to [business.google.com/add](https://business.google.com/add) (or [business.google.com/create](https://business.google.com/create)).
2. Sign in with Google.
3. Choose that customers visit you **in person** (a restaurant with an address).
4. Enter the details from the tables above.
5. Repeat for the second address.

Official help: [Add or claim your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778)

---

## Step 3 — Verify

Google must confirm you run the restaurant. They may ask you to:

- Answer a phone call or text with a code
- Receive a postcard at the restaurant (can take 1–2 weeks)
- Record a short video of the shopfront, sign, and inside

Have someone at the venue who can take a call or film a video.

You cannot fully edit the listing, or reliably appear on Maps, until this is done.

---

## Step 4 — Finish the profile

Once verified, complete both listings:

1. **Photos** — logo, shopfront, dining room, food (dim sum and roast duck). More real photos help.
2. **Description** — short, factual. Example:

   Dim Sum Duck is a walk-in Cantonese restaurant in King's Cross, London. Handmade dim sum and in-house roast duck. Two locations: King's Cross Road and Pentonville Road. Not affiliated with Dim Dou Duck in Kuala Lumpur.

3. **Menu** — link to https://dimsumduck.com/menus
4. **Attributes** — walk-in, dine-in, serves lunch and dinner, alcohol at Pentonville (wine list).
5. **Q&A** — add:
   - “Where are you?” → King's Cross, London, UK. 124 King's Cross Road and 186 Pentonville Road.
   - “Do you take bookings?” → Walk-in only.
   - “Are you Dim Dou Duck in KL?” → No. We are Dim Sum Duck in London.

Reply to reviews when you can.

## How to check it worked

Search Google (from a phone, not logged into the business account if possible):

- Dim Sum Duck London
- Dim Sum Duck King's Cross
- Dim Sum Duck 124 King's Cross Road

You should see your knowledge panel / Maps pin for **London**, not Dim Dou Duck in Malaysia.

This can take a few days after verification. If it still fails after a week, in the Business Profile ask Google to review the listing, and make sure the website, phone, and address match this document.

---

## Please do not

- Put “London” or “best dim sum” inside the **business name** field
- Use a Malaysia address or mix up Dim Dou Duck
- Create extra listings for the same shop
- Use a different phone or website from the one on dimsumduck.com
